PRELIMINARY STATEMENT
1. This is an in rem action brought by the organization of unit owners of a
condominium pursuant to G.L. c. 183A, §§ 6(a) and 6(c), and G.L. c. 254, §§ 5 and 5A, to establish
and enforce a lien for unpaid common expenses. This action further seeks a determination of the

priority of the unit owner organization’s lien pursuant to G.L. c. 183A,

§ 6(c).
JURISDICTION

2. This Court has original jurisdiction over this matter pursuant to G.L. c. 254, § 5.

FACTUAL ALLEGATIONS
6. Pursuant to G.L. c. 183A, § 6, and the applicable provisions of the Condominium’s
governing documents, there is a lien against the Unit in the amount of Four Thousand Nine
Hundred Seventy Eight Dollars and 91/100 ($4,978.91) (hereinafter, “common expenses”).

7. Pursuant to G.L. c. 183A, § 6(a), the lien includes attorneys’ fees and costs incurred

by the Condominium Association in pursuing this matter in accordance with said provisions.

8. Pursuant to, and in accordance with, G.L. c. 183A, § 6(c), the Condominium

Association gave the Defendant notice of said common expenses by certified and first-class mail.

9. Pursuant to G.L. c. 183A, § 6(c), the Condominium Association gave notice to the

First Mortgagee, by certified and first-class mail, of the delinquency and its intention to file this

action.

COUNT I - AGAINST THE UNIT.

10. Pursuant to the provisions of G.L. c. 183A, §§ 6(a) and 6(c), the common expenses
due as aforesaid, and such attorneys’ fees and costs as may additionally accrue hereafter, constitute
a lien upon the Unit.

COUNT II - ESTABLISHING THE PRIORITY OF THE LIEN

11. Pursuant to the provisions of G.L. c. 183A, § 6(c), the lien on the Unit has priority
over all other liens and encumbrances except: (a) liens and encumbrances recorded prior to the
recording of the Master Deed; (b) liens for real estate taxes and other municipal assessments or
charges; and (c) any first mortgage of record.

12. Pursuant to the provisions of G.L. c. 183A, § 6(c), the Condominium Association’s
lien is prior to the first mortgage of record to the extent of monthly common expense assessments
that became due during the six (6) months prior to the institution of this action plus all attorneys’
fees and costs incurred by the Condominium Trust in the enforcement thereof.

PRAYER FOR RELIEF

WHEREFORE, the Condominium Association prays that this Honorable Court enter
Judgment for the Condominium Association and against the Defendant and First Mortgagee as
follows:

(a) _ declare that there is a lien upon the Unit in the amount as may be found;

(b) declare that the lien is entitled to the priority as provided for by law;

(c) order the sale of the Unit to satisfy, in whole or in part, the declared lien; and

(d) grant such other relief as may be just and proper.
