PRELIMINARY STATEMENT
1. This is an in rem action brought by the organization of unit owners of a

condominium pursuant to G.L. c. 183A, §§ 6(a) and 6(c) and G.L. c. 254, §§ 5 and SA, to

establish and enforce a lien for unpaid common expenses. This action further seeks a

determination of the priority of the unit owner organization’s lien under G.L. c. 183A, § 6(c).

JURISDICTION
2. This Court has original jurisdiction over this matter pursuant to G.L. c. 254, § 5.

FACTUAL ALLEGATIONS

6. Pursuant to G.L. c. 183A, § 6, and the applicable provisions of the
Condominium’s governing documents, there is a lien against the Unit in the amount of Six
Thousand Seven Hundred and Twenty-Two Dollars and 13/100 ($6,722.13) (hereinafter,
“common expenses”).

7. Pursuant to G.L. c. 183A, § 6(a), the lien includes attorneys’ fees and costs
incurred by the Condominium Trust in pursuing this matter in accordance with said provisions.

8. Pursuant to G.L. c. 183A, § 6(c), the Condominium Trust gave the Defendant
notice by certified and first-class mail.

9. Pursuant to G.L. c. 183A, § 6(c), the Condominium Trust gave notice to
Wilmington by certified and first-class mail.

COUNT I- AGAINST THE UNIT

10. Pursuant to the provisions of G.L. c. 183A, §§ 6(a) and 6(c), the common
expenses due as aforesaid, and such attorneys’ fees and costs as may additionally accrue
hereafter, constitute liens upon the Unit.

COUNT II - ESTABLISHING THE PRIORITY OF THE LIEN

11. Pursuant to the provisions of G.L. c. 183A, § 6(c), the lien on the Unit has priority
over all other liens and encumbrances except: (a) liens and encumbrances recorded prior to the
recording of the aforesaid Master Deed; (b) liens for real estate taxes and other municipal
assessments or charges; and (c) any first mortgage of record.

12. Pursuant to the provisions of G.L. c. 183A, § 6(c), the Condominium Trust’s lien

is prior to any first mortgage of record to the extent of monthly common expense assessments
