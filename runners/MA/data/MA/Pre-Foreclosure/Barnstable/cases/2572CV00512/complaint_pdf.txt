PRELIMINARY STATEMENT
1. This is an in rem action brought by the organization of unit owners of a

condominium pursuant to G.L. c. 183A, §§ 6(a) and 6(c) and G.L. c. 254, §§ 5 and SA, to
establish and enforce a lien for unpaid common expenses. This action further seeks a
determination of the priority of the unit owner organization’s lien under G.L. c. 183A, § 6(c).
JURISDICTION
2. This Court has original jurisdiction over this matter pursuant to G.L. c. 254, § 5.
PARTIES

3. The Plaintiff, Seashore Point - Deaconess Condominium Trust (“Condominium
Trust”), was established by Declaration of Trust, dated May 22, 2012, and recorded with the
Barnstable County Registry of Deeds (“Registry’’) in Book 26359, Page 245, which
Condominium Trust is the organization of unit owners for Seashore Point — Deaconess

Condominium, a condominium established by Master Deed, dated May 22, 2012, and recorded

FACTUAL ALLEGATIONS

6. Pursuant to G.L. c. 183A, § 6, and the applicable provisions of the
Condominium’s governing documents, there is a lien against the Unit in the amount of Thirty
Seven Thousand Six Hundred Fifty-Three Dollars and 75/100 ($37,653.75) (hereinafter,
“common expenses”).

7. Pursuant to G.L. c. 183A, § 6(a), the lien includes attorneys’ fees and costs

incurred by the Condominium Trust in pursuing this matter in accordance with said provisions.

8. Pursuant to G.L. c. 183A, § 6(c), the Condominium Trust gave the Defendant
notice of said common expenses by certified and first-class mail.
9. There is no mortgage on the Unit.

COUNT I~ AGAINST THE UNIT

10. Pursuant to the provisions of G.L. c. 183A, §§ 6(a) and 6(c), the common
expenses due as aforesaid, and as such attorneys’ fees and costs may additionally accrue
hereafter, constitute a lien upon the Unit.

COUNT II - ESTABLISHING THE PRIORITY OF THE LIEN

11. Pursuant to the provisions of G.L. c. 183A, § 6(c), the lien on the Unit has priority
over all other liens and encumbrances except: (a) liens and encumbrances recorded prior to the
recording of the aforesaid Master Deed; (b) liens for real estate taxes and other municipal
assessments or charges; and (c) any first mortgage of record.

12. Pursuant to the provisions of G.L. c. 183A, § 6(c), the Condominium Trust’s lien
is prior to any first mortgage of record to the extent of monthly common expense assessments
that became due during the six (6) months prior to the institution of this action plus all attorneys’
fees and costs incurred by the Condominium Trust in the enforcement thereof.

PRAYER FOR RELIEF
WHEREFORE, the Condominium Trust prays that this Honorable Court enter judgment
for the Condominium Trust and against the Defendant and Collector as follows:
(a) declare that there is a lien upon the Unit in the amount as may be found;
(b) declare that the lien is entitled to the priority as provided for by law;
(c) order the sale of the Unit to satisfy, in whole or in part, the declared lien; and

(d) grant such other relief as may be just and proper.
