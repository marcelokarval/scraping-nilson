PRELIMINARY STATEMENT
1. This is an in rem action brought by the organization of unit owners of a

condominium pursuant to G.L. c. 183A, §§ 6(a) and 6(c) and G.L. c. 254, §§ 5 and SA, to
establish and enforce a lien for unpaid common expenses. This action further seeks a
determination of the priority of the unit owner organization’s lien under G.L. c. 183A, § 6(c).

FACTUAL ALLEGATIONS
6. Pursuant to G.L. c. 183A, § 6, and the applicable provisions of the
Condominium’s governing documents, there are liens against the Units owned by the Defendant,
which liens collectively total Two Hundred Seventeen Thousand Six Hundred Ninety-Nine
Dollars and 25/100 ($217,699.25) (hereinafter, “common expenses”).
7. Pursuant to G.L. c. 183A, § 6(a), the liens include attorneys’ fees and costs
incurred by the Condominium Trust in pursuing this matter in accordance with said provisions.
8. Pursuant to G.L. c. 183A, § 6(c), the Condominium Trust gave the Defendant
notice of said common expenses by certified and first-class mail.
9. There are no mortgages on the Units.
COUNT I- AGAINST THE UNITS
10. Pursuant to the provisions of G.L. c. 183A, §§ 6(a) and 6(c), the common
expenses due as aforesaid, and such attorneys’ fees and costs as may additionally accrue
hereafter, constitute liens upon the Units.
COUNT II - ESTABLISHING THE PRIORITY OF THE LIENS
11. Pursuant to the provisions of G.L. c. 183A, § 6(c), the liens on the Units have
priority over all other liens and encumbrances except: (a) liens and encumbrances recorded
prior to the recording of the aforesaid Master Deed; (b) liens for real estate taxes and other
municipal assessments or charges; and (c) any first mortgage of record.
12. Pursuant to the provisions of G.L. c. 183A, § 6(c), the Condominium Trust’s lien
is prior to any first mortgage of record to the extent of monthly common expense assessments
that became due during the six (6) months prior to the institution of this action plus all

attorneys’ fees and costs incurred by the Condominium Trust in the enforcement thereof.
