Introduction

This is an action by which the Plaintiff, Citizens Bank, NA f/k/a RBS Citizens NA, seeks authority to
foreclose its mortgage pursuant to the power of sale contained in said mortgage and by entry and
possession, in accordance with the Servicemembers Civil Relief Act and the laws of the Commonwealth of
Massachusetts.

Parties

The Plaintiff, Citizens Bank, NA f/k/a RBS Citizens NA, is a duly incorporated financial institution
authorized to do business in Massachusetts and having its principal place of business at 1 Citizens Plaza,
Providence, RI 02903.

The Defendants, Javier Rodriguez and Ramon O. Rodriguez aka Ramon O. Rodriquez are Massachusetts
residents, with a last and usual place of residence and owner of record at 55 Congress Place, Dedham, MA
02026, consisting of a parcel containing a residence by virtue of a deed recorded with the Norfolk District
Registry of Deeds (the “Registry”) in Book 41474 at Page 35 (the “Vesting Deed”). A true and accurate
copy of the Vesting Deed is attached hereto as Exhibit A.

Development (“HUD”), whose address is 451 Seventh Street SW, Washington, D.C. 20410, has an interest
in this Property by virtue of a Partial Claim Mortgage given by Ramon O. Rodriguez to the Secretary of
Housing and Urban Development, dated June 29, 2024, and recorded with the Norfolk County Registry of
Deeds on July 31, 2024 in Book 41915 at Page 537. No monetary relief is being sought from this
Defendant. See Attached Exhibit B.

The Defendant, Lowell Community Loan Fund, Inc. d/b/a Mill Cities Community Investments, a Non-
Profit Corporation, whose address is 155 Federal Street Suite 700, Boston, MA 02110, has an interest in
this Property by virtue of a Mortgage given by Ramon O. Rodriguez to Mortgage Electronic Registration

from this Defendant. See Attached Exhibit C.
